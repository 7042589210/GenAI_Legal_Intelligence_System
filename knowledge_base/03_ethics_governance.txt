TITLE: Tata Code of Conduct (TCoC) Enforcement
CATEGORY: ETHICS & GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-TCOC-001
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Uphold core ethical values of integrity, responsibility, excellence, and transparency across all business operations and partner engagements.

TITLE: Vendor Code of Conduct (VCoC)
CATEGORY: VENDOR GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-VEND-004
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Mandate third-party adherence to ethical labor standards, environmental protection, safe working conditions, and anti-corruption practices.

TITLE: Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption (ABAC) Policy
CATEGORY: ETHICS & COMPLIANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-ABAC-005
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Enforce zero tolerance for bribery, improper gifts, entertainment expenses, and facilitation payments across all commercial operations.

TITLE: Conflict of Interest Disclosure Policy
CATEGORY: GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-COI-006
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Require employees and business partners to promptly declare any personal, familial, or financial relationships that could compromise objective decision-making.

TITLE: Related Party Transactions (RPT) Review Policy
CATEGORY: FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-RPT-007
JURISDICTION: India
GUIDANCE: Subject all transactions with group affiliates, subsidiaries, or promoters to rigorous audit committee oversight and prior regulatory approval.

TITLE: Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Sourcing Guidelines
CATEGORY: SUSTAINABILITY
REFERENCE_ID: POL-CSR-008
JURISDICTION: India
GUIDANCE: Prioritize sustainable, community-impact driven sourcing practices and allocate procurement budgets to approved social enterprise initiatives.

TITLE: Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Procurement Policy
CATEGORY: SUSTAINABILITY
REFERENCE_ID: POL-ESG-010
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Evaluate suppliers based on carbon footprint reduction metrics, renewable energy usage, and transparent ESG reporting standards.

TITLE: Whistleblower Protection and Reporting Policy
CATEGORY: GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-WBL-012
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Maintain secure, confidential, and anonymous reporting channels for employees and vendors to flag fraud, misconduct, or policy violations.

TITLE: Gift, Hospitality, and Entertainment Thresholds
CATEGORY: ETHICS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-GIFT-016
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Define strict monetary caps, registration mandates, and prior approval workflows for accepting or offering business gifts and entertainment.

TITLE: Tata Enterprise Data Protection & NDA Standard
CATEGORY: CONFIDENTIALITY & COMPLIANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-IND-2026-01
JURISDICTION: India / Global
GUIDANCE: All third-party vendors must adhere strictly to Tata Group confidentiality standards, ensuring encryption at rest and in transit, with mandatory breach notification within 48 hours.

TITLE: Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Mandate
CATEGORY: WORKPLACE GOVERNANCE
REFERENCE_ID: POSH-IND-012
JURISDICTION: India
GUIDANCE: Require vendor organizations working on Tata premises to maintain an active Internal Committee (IC) and enforce zero-tolerance harassment policies.
