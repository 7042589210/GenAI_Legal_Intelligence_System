TITLE: Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) Sourcing Standards
CATEGORY: WORKFORCE & VENDORS
REFERENCE_ID: POL-DEI-009
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Promote supplier diversity by actively onboarding minority-owned, women-owned, and socially inclusive vendor businesses.

TITLE: Human Rights and Labor Standards Framework
CATEGORY: WORKFORCE
REFERENCE_ID: POL-HR-018
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Prohibit child labor, forced labor, discrimination, and unsafe working conditions across all internal facilities and vendor supply chains.

TITLE: Termination for Convenience Clause Standard
CATEGORY: TERMINATION & EXIT
REFERENCE_ID: TERM-CON-102
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Contracts must grant Tata Group the unilateral right to terminate for convenience by providing a 30 to 60 days advance written notice without penalty.

TITLE: Termination for Material Breach & Cure Period
CATEGORY: TERMINATION & EXIT
REFERENCE_ID: TERM-BRC-103
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Immediate termination is permitted upon an un-remedied material breach following a mandatory cure period ranging from 15 to 30 days.

TITLE: Subcontracting Approval and Restriction Policy
CATEGORY: VENDOR MANAGEMENT
REFERENCE_ID: VEND-SUB-303
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Prohibit vendors from subcontracting any part of their contractual obligations without prior written consent from authorized Tata leadership.

TITLE: Exit Management and Transition Assistance Protocol
CATEGORY: TERMINATION & EXIT
REFERENCE_ID: EXIT-TRN-115
JURISDICTION: Global
GUIDANCE: Obligate vendors to provide seamless data migration, knowledge transfer, and operational support for up to 90 days upon contract expiration.
